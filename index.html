walking-on-air

walking-on-air is a personal voice assistant built by Emanuele Fasce for his own use. It connects an Amazon Alexa skill to Claude, so he can ask his Echo device about his day and the recipes of his weekly meal plan.

The app reads, with read-only access, the owner's Google Calendar (to answer questions such as "what do I have tomorrow?" and to show the recipes of planned meals) and files in his Google Drive (such as his monthly goals document).

It is not offered to the public: the only user is its developer.
