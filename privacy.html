Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

claudia is a personal app used only by its developer, Emanuele Fasce. This policy explains how it handles Google user data.

Data accessed

  • Google Calendar (read-only, calendar.readonly): events, times, titles and descriptions.
  • Google Drive (read-only, drive.readonly): the content of documents the owner asks about.

The app cannot create, change or delete anything in Google Calendar or Google Drive.

How the data is used

The data is used only to answer the owner's voice requests on his Alexa device, for example listing tomorrow's events or reading a recipe from a calendar event. To compose an answer, the relevant part of the data is sent to Anthropic's Claude API, which processes it to generate the reply.

Storage and sharing

  • The OAuth token and short-lived caches are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone other than the services needed to answer the request (Anthropic for processing, Amazon Alexa to speak the answer).
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions; the local token can be deleted from the computer running the app.
